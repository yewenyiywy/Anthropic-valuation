# Research methodology and open questions

## Evidence hierarchy
SEC financial disclosures > government administrative series > transaction panel > survey > scenario assumptions. Source links in the CSV refer to primary documents except the JPMorgan analysis of Ramp data. Do not call repeated SEC filings or Ramp-derived charts independent validation.

## Capital recycling
Track ultimate paying customer, channel and funding source separately. Estimate VC-funded share of consumption revenue using invoice-weighted funding provenance, not customer counts. In a funding shock, jointly stress survival, spend and new customer formation. Distinguish cash equity purchases from cloud credits and contractual commitments. A cash flow can be legitimate revenue yet economically dependent on continued financing.

## GTM
For external products, measure pilot -> deployment -> paying customers -> contribution profit. For internal use, measure monetizable savings after integration, inference and oversight costs. Collect revenue-weighted conversion and expansion multipliers. The Gartner and McKinsey samples are not a common cohort.

## Historical checks
BLS historical cohort holdouts provide a weak prior for business survival, not an AI-specific forecast. For diffusion, source ITU/World Bank annual internet/mobile adoption by country and compare logistic versus Gompertz fits on chronological holdouts. Do not infer paid AI spending from historical smartphone adoption. For competition, collect effective price and quality-adjusted cost-per-task rather than model list price alone. These additional historical tests are planned, not completed.

## Identification limits
No verified public country-by-age paid Claude counts, AI-native share of Anthropic revenue, invoice-level funding attribution, precise contract schedule, or empirically estimated joint risk distribution. Use scenarios until available; never silently impute missing observations.
