# Equity-loss development experiment

> **ARCHIVED SURROGATE EXPERIMENT:** This reduced aggregate experiment is not the production model. Do not use its losses to train, calibrate, select, or validate the production architecture. See MODEL_VALIDATION_PROTOCOL.md section 1A.

Frozen design before fitting: train on Cisco, Yahoo, Amazon and eBay (1999–2004), the four original cohort members with matching revenue and market-cap endpoints assembled here. This is a survivor-selected retrospective subset, not a point-in-time sample. Other original issuers remain unscored; do not treat bankruptcy, merger or missing observations as equivalent. Cloud 2020–2025 is a previously exposed chronological transfer diagnostic, not a final test. Fiscal reports may postdate calendar-end anchors. No release claim.

Use the shared joint engine's aggregate restriction with company-specific revenue growth and CFO minus capex margin. Neutral inactive axes are not estimated customer states. CFO minus capex is an imperfect levered cash-flow proxy (including SBC addback); do not claim FCFF or full FCFE. Exclude unidentified excess cash, debt and net borrowing consistently; discount at an illustrative fixed 12%. Fit growth shrinkage (0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1), mature proxy margin (0.10, 0.15, 0.25, 0.35, 0.45), and finite lifecycle horizon (20, 30, 40 years). Margin converges linearly over ten years; constant tail transitions are not estimated. Baseline (0.5, 0.15, 30). Objective mean absolute log1p equity error plus 0.02 squared normalized distance from baseline (scales 0.5, 0.15, 10). Select on training only; report all grid trials, both subsets, fixed unchanged-market-cap benchmark, revenue error and directional bias. No Anthropic parameter promotion; no final blind test access. Dollar amounts USD billions.

Second development experiment (after observing first diagnostic): separate market price from intrinsic cash-flow proxy. Freeze mature margin and horizon at baseline; fit growth shrinkage by training revenue log error alone. Fit persistence of initial log market premium, grid 0 to 1 by .1, against training five-year market-cap log error. No use of transfer labels in either fit. This is a market-price forecast conditional on observed initial price, not independent intrinsic valuation. Transfer set has now been examined repeatedly and remains development-only.
